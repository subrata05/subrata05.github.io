This is a starter article. Replace it with your own writing before treating it as a published post.

Reliable firmware is not only about making the normal path work. It is also about understanding timing, defining what happens when something fails, and making behaviour observable during testing.

When documenting a project, I try to answer three questions:

1. What assumptions does the design make?
2. How can I verify those assumptions on real hardware?
3. What information will help someone debug the system later?

A useful technical note does not need to be long. A clear problem, a short explanation of the approach, and evidence from testing can be enough to help another engineer.
